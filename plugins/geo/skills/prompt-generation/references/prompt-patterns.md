# Prompt Patterns

Use these as starting points. Replace placeholder language with the audience's actual words.

## TOFU

- how do [audience] [accomplish outcome]
- what is [concept] and does my [company or situation] need it
- difference between [option a] and [option b]
- why do [buyers/users] struggle with [problem]

## MOFU

- i need [category] that [solves pain point]
- looking for [category] that lets me [desired outcome]
- is there a [category] for [specific audience or situation]
- how can i [outcome] without [undesirable tradeoff]
- what should i look for in a [category] for [use case]

## BOFU

- best [category] for [specific use case]
- best [category] for [industry or vertical]
- [competitor] alternatives for [specific reason]
- [brand] vs [competitor] for [use case]
- cheapest way to [outcome] without [tradeoff]
- switching from [competitor] because [pain point]

## Branded Baseline

Use branded prompts only when the tracker needs a reputation or entity baseline:

- is [brand] good for [use case]
- what does [brand] do
- [brand] alternatives
- [brand] pricing
- [brand] reviews
