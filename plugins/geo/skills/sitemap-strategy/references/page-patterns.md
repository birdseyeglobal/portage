# Citation-Winning Page Patterns

These patterns recur across AI-search citation graphs. Use them as diagnostic lenses, not templates to force onto every brand.

## Location Page

Useful for local services, recruiting, healthcare, legal, home services, education, and marketplaces.

Common sections:

- City or region in the H1.
- Service coverage and neighborhoods.
- Local proof: address, team, licenses, examples, testimonials.
- Service list.
- FAQ with local questions.
- LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList schema when appropriate.

## Service or Practice Hub

Useful when prompts cluster around a service category.

Common sections:

- Problem and audience fit.
- Sub-services or use cases.
- Process or methodology.
- Proof points.
- Related resources.
- FAQ.

## Comparison Page

Useful when buyers ask about alternatives, migration, or vendor selection.

Common sections:

- Honest comparison criteria.
- Side-by-side table.
- Where each option fits.
- Migration or switching notes.
- FAQ.

## Vertical or Use-Case Page

Useful for B2B SaaS, fintech, agencies, marketplaces, and data products.

Common sections:

- Audience-specific pain.
- Workflow or use-case map.
- Relevant integrations or constraints.
- Customer proof.
- Implementation notes.

## Trust Page

Useful when AI answers cite entity authority.

Common sections:

- Company overview.
- Leadership and credentials.
- Awards, licensing, certifications, or press.
- Methodology.
- Contact and social profiles.
- Organization, Person, sameAs, and review-related schema where appropriate.

## Resource or Answer Page

Useful when AI answers need a direct answer to a fact-based query.

Common sections:

- Direct answer near the top.
- Definitions.
- Steps or criteria.
- Examples.
- Sources.
- FAQ.
